### Year in Review {#year-in-review}

**2025** was a year of steady growth and meaningful milestones in my professional journey. I had the opportunity to reconnect with the bioprocess community through AMBIC and CCE meetings. I was honored to receive the _Martin Sinacore Young Investigator Award_ at CCE and to deliver the award lecture remotely, an experience that highlighted the collective nature of the work and the mentors, collaborators, and teams who have shaped my path. Within GSK, I continued to focus on translating Raman spectroscopy, model predictive control, and other digital twin applications into practical process improvements and control. These exciting contributions were recognized with an unexpected _Transformational Medicines and Vaccines Excellence Award_.
